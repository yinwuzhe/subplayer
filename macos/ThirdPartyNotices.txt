SubPlayer Mac 1.1 — Third-party playback components

This application embeds unmodified libVLC/libvlccore and playback modules from
VideoLAN VLC 3.0.24 (macOS arm64). It does not require VLC to be installed.
The original library signatures are replaced with local ad-hoc signatures for
bundling. The VLC macOS application-interface plugin is not included.

VLC and its components are copyright their respective VideoLAN contributors.
libVLC and libvlccore are licensed under LGPL 2.1 or later. Individual modules
and bundled dependencies retain their own licenses, including GPL and LGPL.
The accompanying COPYING, COPYING.LIB and AUTHORS files are from VLC 3.0.24.

The libraries and modules remain dynamically linked in Contents/Frameworks/VLC.
They can be replaced with ABI-compatible builds; the local application signature
must then be renewed. No DRM or library-validation restriction is added.

This local build is not Apple-notarized. Before public redistribution, review
and comply with the license and corresponding-source obligations of every
included module and dependency.
